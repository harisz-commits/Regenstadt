Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a corporate executive office at the top of a
tower at night — one desk, one occupant, an entire wall of glass. Photoreal,
cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: a vast dark stone floor, almost empty. Dead centre against a full
height window wall, a long low desk of black stone, and behind it ONE woman in
her fifties in a dark tailored suit, standing rather than seated, turned to
face the camera, hands loosely at her sides. She is entirely calm and has been
expecting this. Beyond the glass, the rain-lit city drops away far below. On
the LEFT a low cabinet with a single closed folder on it. On the RIGHT a
seating group nobody has ever used.

LIGHT: almost none from the room itself — the city through the glass is the
light source, cold blue and distant amber, so she is mostly a silhouette with
one side of her face caught. A single small desk lamp, off.

MOOD: the top of the chain, and it is one person in an empty room.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no other people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no
company name. Not an illustration, not anime.
