Repaint this as a WIDER, BUSIER city street at night in heavy rain — the main
road that the narrow alley opens onto. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the horizon line at the same height, the centred
vanishing point, the wet asphalt receding straight ahead, the same weather and
the same warm-sodium-plus-cold-neon lighting scheme.

CHANGE: the walls pull far back — this is a broad street, not an alley. Traffic
crosses left to right in the middle distance, headlights and tail lights

buildings towards a blue-lit official building. On the RIGHT edge, a doorway
under an old illuminated sign. Near the left foreground a small glazed kiosk,
lit from inside, stacked with goods. On the right an overhead notice board with
layers of pasted paper.

The left and right edges must each read clearly as a way to go — an opening,
not a wall.

LIGHT: warm sodium overhead, cold neon spill from shopfronts, cold blue cast
from the far left. Reflections stretched long on the wet road.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no readable text, no watermark, no logo. Faces never in focus.
