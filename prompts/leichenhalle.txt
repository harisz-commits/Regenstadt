Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a municipal morgue — a cold storage room of
body drawers. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir. Restrained and clinical, not
gruesome.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: left and right, walls of stainless steel drawer fronts in three tiers,
each with a recessed handle and an empty card holder. Down the centre, a
polished concrete floor with a shallow drainage channel. In the middle
distance on the left, ONE drawer stands pulled halfway out and is EMPTY — clean
steel, nothing on it. Against the far wall a wheeled steel trolley, bare. A
scale hangs from the ceiling to one side. Everything else closed.

LIGHT: cold white ceiling panels, one flickering, reflecting hard off the steel
and the wet floor. Faint mist of cold air near the floor. No warm tones
anywhere. No rain indoors.

MOOD: tidy, refrigerated, and short one body.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no bodies, no blood, no gore, no readable text, no
watermark, no logo. Not an illustration, not anime.
