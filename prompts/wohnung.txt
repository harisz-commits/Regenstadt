Repaint this into the INTERIOR of the investigator's own flat at night — one
room, high above a rainy street, lived in and not tidied. Photoreal,
cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: dark parquet, a worn armchair turned away from the camera on the
right, a low table with a glass and an overflowing ashtray. Dead centre in the
far wall, a tall window running floor to ceiling, rain streaming down it, the
city beyond reduced to smeared amber and blue. To the LEFT of the window, a
large cork pinboard covering most of the wall, empty except for a few pins and
one photograph — the surface waiting to be filled. On the far right a
kitchenette recess with a single lit bulb. Papers on the floor near the chair.

LIGHT: mostly from the window — cold city glow, rain shadows moving across the
floor. One warm bulb in the kitchenette. Everything else dark. No rain indoors.

MOOD: the one room in the city where nobody is watching.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no readable
writing on the pinboard. Not an illustration, not anime.
