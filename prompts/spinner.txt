Repaint this as a view from INSIDE a flying car, high above a rain-soaked
future city at night, looking out and slightly down over the skyline.
Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP: the centred vanishing point and the sense of great depth receding away
from the camera.

THE VIEW: we are above the weather layer looking down through it. Dense towers
below, their tops lit, their bases lost in orange haze. Traffic lanes of other
flying vehicles as strings of light threading between the towers at several
heights. Far below, the grid of streets glows amber through cloud. Rain streaks
across the canopy glass in the immediate foreground, sharp and out of focus,
lit from within by faint instrument glow along the bottom edge of frame.

LIGHT: cold blue-grey above, warm amber haze below, the sodium glow of the city
bouncing off the cloud deck. High contrast.

MOOD: enormous, indifferent, beautiful. The city seen the way only someone who
can leave it sees it.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no cockpit HUD lettering.
Not an illustration, not anime, no painterly brushwork.
