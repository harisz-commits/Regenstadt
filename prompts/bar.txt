Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a small, dark, nameless bar, seen from just
inside the entrance. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: a long zinc-topped bar running from the left foreground into the
middle distance, its surface polished bright in two worn patches. Behind it a
shelf of bottles lit from below — one gap in an otherwise unbroken row. On the
right, deep booth seating in cracked dark leather; the furthest booth is empty
and its seat is visibly wet. Far left, an old jukebox glowing, its glass dusty
except for one clean patch. Far right at the back, a service door standing
slightly ajar with cold light behind it.

ONE PERSON: a woman behind the bar, standing at the near end where the zinc is
worn brightest, mid-forties, dark sleeveless top, forearms on the counter,
looking straight down the bar towards the camera. Lit from below by the bottle
shelf so her face is warm and her eyes are in shadow. She is not welcoming and
not hostile. She is waiting for you to say something.

LIGHT: warm low amber from behind the bar and from small shaded lamps over the
booths, cold blue-green spill from the jukebox and from the gap of the back
door. Almost everything else in shadow. Smoke or haze hanging in the light. No
rain indoors, no puddles.

MOOD: a room where people come to not be found.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no other people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo.
Not an illustration, not anime, no painterly brushwork.
