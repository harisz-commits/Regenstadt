Repaint this into the INTERIOR of an illegal back-room clinic hidden beneath the
city — a treatment room that is not on any register. Photoreal, cinematic,
neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: a low vaulted brick cellar converted with plastic sheeting hung as
walls. Dead centre, an old operating table under a battered surgical lamp — the
only thing in the room that is clean. On the left, a steel trolley with
instruments laid out on a cloth, and a stack of unmarked drug cartons. On the
right, an improvised rack of medical equipment cabled together, and a chair
with restraint straps hanging open. Buckets on the floor. A drain in the
concrete beneath the table.

LIGHT: the surgical lamp is the only strong source, a hard white cone straight
down onto the table, everything beyond it falling into brown-black shadow. A
faint green glow from the equipment rack. No rain, no puddles.

MOOD: somebody was treated here recently, and it was not their choice.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no blood, no gore, no readable text, no watermark, no
logo. Not an illustration, not anime.
