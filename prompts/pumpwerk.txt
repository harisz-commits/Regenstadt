Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a vast underground pumping station — a
cathedral-sized machine hall far below the city. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The hall recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE HALL: enormous cast-iron pump housings in two receding rows, left and
right, taller than a person, riveted, streaked with rust and mineral deposits.
Between them a wet steel walkway with a handrail, running straight to the far
wall where a huge sluice gate stands closed. Water sheets down the walls in
places and pools on the floor. Thick pipes overhead. A spiral stair on the left
going further down. On the right, a control desk with dead dials, one lamp lit.

LIGHT: sparse caged bulbs high up, cold and yellow-green, throwing long
shadows between the pump housings. Steam drifting at floor level. Deep black
between the machines.

MOOD: it has been running unattended for decades and does not care who is here.
