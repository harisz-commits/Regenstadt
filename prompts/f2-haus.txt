Repaint this render as a wide shot INSIDE one of the small works houses at
night, in deep winter — the home of a man who is officially dead.

A single low room: a mattress on the floor with too many blankets, a paraffin
stove, a table made from a door on two crates. On the table a radio, a tin
plate, and a stack of newspapers a year old. The window is covered from the
inside with cardboard, taped, with one small gap left at eye height.

On the wall, dozens of tally marks scratched into the plaster in groups of
five. A coat and two more coats on a single nail.

A MAN IS SITTING ON THE MATTRESS, clearly visible, in two overcoats with the
collars up, elbows on his knees, hands hanging between them. He is looking
towards the doorway the camera stands in. Not a silhouette — his face and
posture must read at this distance. He is the only living thing in the room
and the eye must find him first.

One warm bulb and the glow of the stove; cold blue leaking through the gap in
the cardboard. Indoors: dry, close, stale.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white. Wide establishing
shot from standing eye height, the vanishing point near the centre of frame.

FORBIDDEN: absolutely no text, no watermark, no logo, no readable letters or
numbers anywhere. No faces towards the camera. Not an illustration, not anime.
