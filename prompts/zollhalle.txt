Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a customs inspection hall — a long corrugated
freight shed at night. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The hall recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE HALL: bare concrete floor with painted lane markings worn to nothing. Left
and right, long steel inspection tables running away from camera, crates opened
and left open on them, packing straw and torn banding on the floor. High
corrugated walls, a roller shutter closed at the far end. Suspended from the
ceiling, a row of caged work lamps, most of them off. On the left wall a rack of
clipboards on hooks. On the right, a wheeled scanning arch, powered down.

LIGHT: two working lamps only, one over the near left table and one far down the
hall, so the middle of the shed is nearly dark. Cold blue-white. Faint orange
sodium leaking under the roller shutter. No rain indoors, no puddles.

MOOD: a place that was busy an hour ago and was left in a hurry.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no readable
labels on crates. Not an illustration, not anime.
