cabinet, and eight formica tables under fluorescent tubes — three of the tubes
dead, so the light falls in bands. Chairs upside down on most tables. At one
table near the window a man sits alone with his back half to the camera, a cap
on the table beside a mug.

The window wall looks onto the snowed-in yard. A noticeboard by the door with
nothing pinned to it but drawing pins.

Cold institutional green-white from the tubes, one warm patch at the hot
cabinet. Indoors: dry, condensation on the window.
