Repaint this render as a wide shot INSIDE the boardroom of a state authority,
high above the city, at night in deep winter.

A long room. On the RIGHT, a floor-to-ceiling panorama window looking down over
a snowed-in city; far out in the dark plain stands ONE single burning
floodlight, small and hard, near the middle of the pane. A long table runs down
the room, chairs pushed in except one, which stands drawn back and facing the
window.

On the TABLE, centre of frame, a reel-to-reel tape machine, plugged in and
RUNNING, both reels turning, with a stack of tape boxes beside it. On the LEFT
WALL, a large framed technical CROSS-SECTION drawing, two metres wide: layers
of ground, shafts and depths, and at the very bottom a hatched body inside the
rock around which everything else is drawn. The hatching has been extended
several times in a different pencil.

At the near left end of the table, a stack of leather-bound folders.

A MAN IN A DARK SUIT STANDS AT THE WINDOW ON THE RIGHT, clearly visible, seen
from behind and slightly to the side, hands behind his back, very upright,
looking down at the city. Not a silhouette — he must read as a person.

Cold blue from the window, one warm pool of light over the table.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white. Wide establishing
shot from standing eye height, the vanishing point near the centre of frame.

FORBIDDEN: absolutely no text, no watermark, no logo, no readable letters or
numbers anywhere — the cross-section drawing carries no labels, no scale, no
figures, only lines and hatching. No faces towards the camera. Not an
illustration, not anime.
