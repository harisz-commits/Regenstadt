Repaint this render as a wide shot INSIDE the mortuary of a police
headquarters at night, in winter.

A tiled basement room. On the LEFT, a wall of stainless steel body drawers,
three rows; ONE drawer in the middle row is pulled half out, and on its tray
lies a body under a sheet, only the shape visible, the face covered. In the
CENTRE, a rolling trolley, empty and bright, left standing in the aisle with
its castors turned. On the RIGHT, another bank of closed steel drawers, and a
hanging scale above. Along the floor, a drainage channel; beside it a shallow
enamel tray holding folded wet clothing.

AN OLD MAN IN A LONG GREY COAT STANDS AT THE OPEN DRAWER, right of it, clearly
visible, seen in three-quarter view from behind and the side, very upright,
hands clasped behind his back, looking down at what lies there. Not a
silhouette — he must read as a person. No one else in the room.

Cold blue-white overhead light, hard reflections on steel and tile. Indoors:
no rain, dry, breath-cold air.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white. Wide establishing
shot from standing eye height, the vanishing point near the centre of frame.

FORBIDDEN: absolutely no text, no watermark, no logo, no readable letters or
numbers anywhere — no labels in the card holders on the drawers. No faces
towards the camera, no visible face at all. No gore, no blood, no exposed
body — the body stays fully covered. Not an illustration, not anime.
