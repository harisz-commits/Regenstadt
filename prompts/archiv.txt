Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a corporate records archive — a long, cold
room of compact shelving, deep inside a company building. Photoreal,
cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ARCHIVE: left and right, tall rolling compact-shelving units with hand
wheels on their ends, closed tight except for ONE aisle cranked open in the
middle distance on the left, its interior dark. The shelves are packed with
identical grey box files. Down the centre, a narrow polished floor lane to the
far wall, where a single reading terminal sits on a steel desk, its screen on
and casting light. Above, a run of cold ceiling tubes, every second one dead.
On the right foreground, a low trolley with a few boxes pulled and stacked
crookedly, out of place in a room where nothing else is out of place.

LIGHT: cold institutional white from the tubes, a colder blue from the terminal
screen at the far end, deep shadow inside the opened aisle. No rain, no
puddles.

MOOD: everything here is kept, and none of it is meant to be read.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no readable
labels on the boxes. Not an illustration, not anime.
