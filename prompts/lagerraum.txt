Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a small industrial storage room, seen from a
person standing just inside the door. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: bare concrete floor with old oil stains, breeze-block walls, a
steel-framed workbench on the left with a vice, tools and metal swarf. On the
right a tall industrial shelving rack loaded with jerrycans, cable drums and
wooden crates — one shelf conspicuously empty and clean where something was
removed. Against the back right wall a folding camp bed with a blanket thrown
back. A single fluorescent tube hangs from two wires near the ceiling, slightly
askew, and is the ONLY strong light source.

LIGHT: harsh, cold, top-down from that one tube. Long hard shadows under the
bench and rack. Deep unlit corners. A thin warm sliver of sodium light leaks
under the door behind the camera. No rain, no wet reflections, no puddles —
this is indoors and dry.

MOOD: someone has been living in here and did not want it known.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no text, no watermark, no logo, no readable letters.
Not an illustration, not anime, no painterly brushwork.
