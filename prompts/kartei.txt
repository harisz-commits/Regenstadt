Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a paper card-index room — a narrow archive of
index cabinets, deeper inside a municipal building. Photoreal, cinematic,
neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The room recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE ROOM: left and right, floor-to-ceiling banks of small wooden card drawers
with brass pulls, hundreds of them, receding into the distance. Down the centre
a narrow aisle barely wide enough for one person. In the near foreground on the
right, ONE drawer pulled fully out and set on top of the cabinet, its cards
fanned and disturbed — the only disorder in the room. A wooden library ladder
on a rail leans against the left bank. At the far end, a small window of
frosted glass with a streetlamp behind it.

LIGHT: a single bare bulb on a cord in the middle of the aisle, warm and weak,
falling off fast; cold blue-grey from the frosted window at the far end. Deep
shadow between the cabinets. Dust visible in the light. No rain indoors.

MOOD: everything about everyone, on paper, and nobody has come in here for
years — until two days ago.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no readable
labels on the drawers or cards. Not an illustration, not anime.
