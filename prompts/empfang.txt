Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a corporate reception hall — the ground floor
lobby of a powerful company, late at night. Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The hall recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE HALL: polished dark stone floor with a mirror sheen, reflecting the lights
above. Dead centre in the far wall, a long unbroken reception counter of pale
backlit stone. Behind it stands ONE woman in a dark uniform jacket, mid-thirties,
hands flat on the counter, looking directly down the hall at the camera —
composed, unwelcoming, entirely still. To her left and right, the far wall is
sheer polished panel. On the LEFT of the hall, a bank of steel elevator doors,
one with a lit call panel. On the RIGHT, a low seating island of black leather
and a single tall plant, and beyond it a narrower side corridor leading away.
The ceiling is high and dark with a grid of recessed lights.

The left elevators and the right corridor must each read clearly as a way to go.

LIGHT: cold white from the recessed grid and from under the counter, a colder
blue-green from the elevator panel. Everything expensive, nothing warm. Long
reflections on the stone floor. No rain indoors, no puddles.

MOOD: money that does not have to be polite.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no other people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no
company name anywhere. Not an illustration, not anime.
