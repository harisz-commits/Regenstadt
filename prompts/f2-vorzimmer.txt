Repaint this render as a wide shot INSIDE the anteroom of a senior state office
at night, in winter.

Panelled walls, worn parquet, a heavy carpet. In the CENTRE, a secretary's desk
with a typewriter, an open appointment book and a shaded brass lamp; behind it,
in the far centre wall, a pair of DOUBLE DOORS twice as tall as necessary,
standing slightly ajar with bright, still light behind them.

On the LEFT, a bank of file cabinets: four drawers, three with old worn locks,
the lowest with a new bright lock and no label plate. On the RIGHT, a lit glass
display case on a plinth, and inside it, on black velvet, ONE pale hand-length
drill core lying like a relic. On the desk corner, a leather dispatch folder.

A MAN IN A GOOD SUIT STANDS BESIDE THE DESK, left of centre, clearly visible in
three-quarter view, a folder under his arm, positioned between the camera and
the double doors. Not a silhouette — he must read as a person.

Warm lamp light against cold blue from the window. Indoors: no rain.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white. Wide establishing
shot from standing eye height, the vanishing point near the centre of frame.

FORBIDDEN: absolutely no text, no watermark, no logo, no readable letters or
