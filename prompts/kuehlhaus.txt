Repaint this into the INTERIOR of a refrigerated warehouse on the docks — a
cold store of stacked crates, far too cold for people. Photoreal, cinematic,
neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the camera height, the horizontal line where floor
meets far wall, and the centred vanishing point. The hall recedes straight
ahead exactly as the reference does.

THE HALL: left and right, high stacks of insulated crates on pallets, frosted
white at the edges. A steel-grid floor with drainage. Cold vapour lying at
knee height across the whole floor. Down the centre an aisle to a heavy
insulated door at the far end, its rubber seal split, held shut by a bar. On
the LEFT in the middle distance, a small partitioned office of plywood and
frosted glass, lit warm from inside — the only warmth in the picture, clearly
not part of the original building. A folding chair and a thermos outside its
door.

LIGHT: cold blue-white ceiling fittings, half of them off, everything else
lost in vapour. One warm rectangle from the plywood office. Frost catching the
light on every edge.

MOOD: somebody has been keeping something in here that is not cargo.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo. Not an
illustration, not anime.
