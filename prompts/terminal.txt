Repaint this as a FREIGHT TERMINAL at night in heavy rain — a working cargo
quay on black water, seen from a person standing on the loading apron.
Photoreal, cinematic, neo-noir.

KEEP from the reference: the horizon line at the same height, the centred
vanishing point, the wet ground receding straight ahead, the same weather and
the same warm-sodium-plus-cold-neon lighting scheme.

CHANGE: this is open, not enclosed. Left and right are stacked shipping
containers in long receding rows, forming a canyon instead of buildings. Down
the centre, a wet loading apron runs to a gantry crane silhouetted against the
sky. Beyond it, black water and the lights of the far bank. On the LEFT, a
freight door in a corrugated hall stands open, warm light inside. On the RIGHT,
a customs booth with a lowered barrier, cold blue lamp above it. In the near
foreground, pallets under torn plastic sheeting, puddles between them.

The left and right edges must each read clearly as a way to go.

LIGHT: harsh sodium floodlights on masts, cold blue from the customs booth,
everything else in deep wet shadow. Long reflections in standing water.

LOOK: photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 35mm, Blade Runner 2049.
Fine film grain. Highlights roll off, never clip to white.

FORBIDDEN: no people, no readable text, no watermark, no logo, no container
markings that read as letters. Not an illustration, not anime.
